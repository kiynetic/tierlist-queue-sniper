## 2026-10-14 - Accessibility: ARIA Labels and Form Associations\n**Learning:** Discovered that several modals use icon-only close buttons lacking `aria-label` attributes, and several form `<label>` elements lack `for` attributes, making them inaccessible to screen readers and difficult to focus via clicking the label.\n**Action:** Ensure all icon-only buttons receive descriptive `aria-label` attributes and all form labels are explicitly associated with their input fields using the `for` attribute matching the input's `id`.
